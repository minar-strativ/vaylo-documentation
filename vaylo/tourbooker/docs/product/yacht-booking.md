# Yacht Booking

Yacht charters are a separate booking type alongside tours and hotels — a catalog of chartered yachts, synced in from external charter providers, that customers can search, price out, and book directly. This capability is split into two areas, covered in their own docs:

- **[Yacht Catalog & Pricing](yacht-booking-catalog-and-pricing.md)** — the catalog of available yachts and where they operate (companies, countries, sailing areas, shipyards), manually-added boats, and per-week pricing management.
- **[Yacht Search, Booking & Reservation](yacht-booking-booking-and-reservation.md)** — how a customer searches for and books a yacht, and how that booking is confirmed and managed with the external charter provider through to departure.

## Related Features

- [Bookings](booking.md) — a yacht booking uses the same underlying booking record as tour and hotel bookings.
- [Passengers](passengers.md) — the travelers on a yacht booking are recorded the same way as on any other booking.
