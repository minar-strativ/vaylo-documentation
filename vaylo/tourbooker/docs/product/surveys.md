# Surveys

Surveys automates collecting post-tour feedback. A survey is built on an external survey platform and attached to a tour; once the tour returns, every traveling passenger with an email address is automatically sent a personal link to fill it out.

## What it does

- Surveys are created on an external survey platform (Netigate) and synced in so staff can attach one to a tour.
- The day before a tour returns, the platform automatically gathers every traveling passenger's details from that tour's bookings and creates a personalized batch ("sendout") of survey links on the survey platform.
- A day after the sendout is created, each passenger is emailed their own personal survey link.
- The status of each sendout is tracked so staff can see whether its follow-up email has gone out yet.

## Who uses it

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| Staff/Admin | Sync the list of surveys from the survey platform and view them; attach a survey to a tour. |
| System | Automatically creates a tour's survey sendout ahead of its return date, and emails each respondent their personal link on schedule. |

Customers only ever receive the resulting survey email — they have no other interaction with this feature.

## How it works

1. Staff (or an automatic sync) pull the list of surveys from the survey platform into the tenant, and attach the relevant one to a tour.
2. The day before that tour's return date, the platform collects every traveling passenger with an email address from the tour's bookings — along with tour context like departure date, destination, tour guide, and skipper — and sends it to the survey platform to create a personalized sendout.
3. Each respondent's own personal survey link, generated by the survey platform, is recorded against that sendout.
4. 24 hours after the sendout was created, a scheduled job emails each respondent their personal survey link, then marks the sendout as sent.

## Rules & Edge Cases

- Both the sendout creation and the follow-up email require the tenant's Netigate survey subscription add-on to be active — without it, both scheduled jobs report that the subscription is inactive and do nothing.
- Only passengers who are actually traveling on a booking, and who have a real email address, are included in a sendout.
- Each respondent only ever gets one survey email per sendout, even if they appear more than once in the respondent list.

## Limitations

- Surveys themselves are built and managed entirely on the external survey platform — this feature only syncs their list and attaches one to a tour; it doesn't let staff design questions or view responses.
- Only tours with a survey explicitly attached generate a sendout; there's no default or fallback survey.

## FAQ

**Q: What happens if a tour has no survey attached?**
A: Nothing — only tours with a survey explicitly attached get a sendout created for them.

**Q: Can a passenger without an email address be surveyed?**
A: No — only traveling passengers with a real email address are included.

**Q: How soon after a tour returns does the survey email go out?**
A: The sendout is created the day before the tour's return date, and the follow-up email goes out 24 hours after that.

**Q: What if the tenant hasn't subscribed to the survey add-on?**
A: Both the sendout and the follow-up email steps do nothing and report that the subscription isn't active.
