# Glossary — Canonical Business Terms

One concept, one term, everywhere. Docs must use these exact terms. When analysis surfaces a new business concept, add it here in the same step — a term not in the glossary does not go in a doc.

Format: **Term** — definition in one or two sentences, primary-reader language. Add "(not: ...)" for banned synonyms.

---

**Draft** — A tour that is not yet ready to sell: always inactive and hidden from customers until taken out of Draft. (not: unpublished, pending)

**Capacity Hold** — A temporary reduction in a tour's available capacity while a booking is being completed, which auto-releases if not confirmed by its expiry date. (not: reservation, lock)

**Departed** — A tour whose departure date has passed and whose automatic status rollover has run; does not mean cancelled or deleted.

**Allotment Tour** — A tour that sells room-type inventory in bulk blocks shared with the Hotels & Accommodation feature, rather than by passenger capacity alone.

**Fixed Allotment** — A room-type's contractually committed room inventory for a date, available regardless of demand. Booked before Call-off inventory.

**Call-off Allotment** — Extra room-type inventory available on request for a date, beyond the Fixed allotment. Only booked once Fixed is exhausted.
